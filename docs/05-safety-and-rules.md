# Safety and rules

Riders must be at least 16 years old. Helmets are not required by the city, but they are recommended, and the kiosks of the larger stations lend them for free.

The lights of the bikes switch on by themselves when you start pedalling. If a light does not work, end the ride at the next station and report the bike.

Ride on the bike lanes where there is one. In the pedestrian zone of the old town, walk the bike between 10 am and 7 pm.

If you have an accident or find a bike left outside a station, call the service line written on every station. It answers every day from 5 am to 1 am.
