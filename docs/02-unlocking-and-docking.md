# Unlocking and docking

If the bike does not come out of the dock after the green light, lift the rear wheel a little and pull again within 30 seconds. After 30 seconds the dock locks the bike again and you need to scan it a second time.

A blinking yellow light means that the dock is checking your account. A red light means that the bike cannot be released: the dock shows an error code on its small screen, and the app shows the same code.

When a station is full, the app shows the nearest stations with free docks and gives you 15 extra minutes at no cost to reach one of them.

Electric bikes have a battery level on the dock screen. Choose a bike with at least two bars for rides longer than 20 minutes.
