# Passes and prices

A single ride costs 1.50 for the first 30 minutes. A day pass costs 6 and includes any number of rides of up to 45 minutes each for 24 hours. A monthly pass costs 25 and includes rides of up to 45 minutes.

Electric bikes cost 0.10 more for every minute, also with a pass.

If you keep a bike longer than your ride or your pass allows, every extra 15 minutes costs 2. After 24 hours the bike counts as lost and the account is charged 400.

Refunds for a ride cut short by a broken bike or dock are automatic when you report the problem in the app before you start another ride.
