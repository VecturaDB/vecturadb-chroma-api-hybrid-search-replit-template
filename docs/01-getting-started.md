# Getting started

Spokeford Bikes is a fictional bike-share service. There are 140 docking stations and about 1,600 bikes, half of them electric. The service runs every day from 5 am to 1 am.

To ride, create an account in the Spokeford Bikes app with a phone number and a payment card. You can also buy a day pass at the kiosk of any station without an account.

Your first ride starts at any station: choose a bike, scan the QR code on its handlebar with the app, and wait for the green light on the dock. Pull the bike backwards out of the dock.

The ride ends when you push the bike into a free dock at any station until the light turns green. The app shows the length and the price of the ride a few seconds later.
